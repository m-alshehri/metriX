metriX v5.2 — Vercel Hobby Cron Fix
===================================

Cause:
The v5.1 repository had /api/cron/provider-retries scheduled hourly:
15 * * * *
Vercel Hobby only permits each Cron Job to run once per day.

Fixed schedules:
- /api/cron/metrix-pipeline -> 0 21 * * *  = 00:00 Saudi time
- /api/cron/provider-retries -> 15 21 * * * = 00:15 Saudi time

No Supabase SQL changes are required.
