metriX v4.3 cron update
=======================

The daily pipeline cron is now scheduled at 21:00 UTC every day.
Saudi Arabia is UTC+3, so this runs at exactly 00:00 (midnight) Saudi time.

Cron expression: 0 21 * * *
Route: /api/cron/metrix-pipeline

The cron route loads all projects from the projects table.
A project is skipped only when its project_settings row exists and automation_enabled is false.
Projects with automation enabled, or with no settings row, are processed.
