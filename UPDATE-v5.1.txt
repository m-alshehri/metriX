metriX v5.1 — typography, social icons and language switcher
=============================================================

CHANGES
1) Tajawal is now the global font across the English and Arabic metriX UI.
   Included weights: 200, 300, 400, 500, 700, 800, 900.
   Implemented with Next.js next/font/google.

2) Social platform marks are no longer hand-drawn approximations.
   PlatformIcon now renders the Simple Icons v16 SVG artwork for:
   X, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Threads, Facebook, LinkedIn,
   Google Maps, Reddit and Snapchat.
   Existing grayscale/currentColor styling remains controllable from the UI.

3) Language indicator:
   - English UI shows the Saudi flag 🇸🇦 to switch to Arabic.
   - Arabic UI shows the UK flag 🇬🇧 to switch to English.

No Supabase SQL changes are required for this update.
